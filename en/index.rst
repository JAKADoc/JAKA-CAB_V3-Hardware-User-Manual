JAKA |product_name| Hardware User Manual
*****************************************

The JAKA |product_name| is a robot control cabinet featuring a wide range of interfaces and high-level safety. 
It is available in four models: CAB L, CAB H, CAB LiteAC, and CAB LiteDC. 
Among them, CAB L, CAB H, and CAB LiteAC are standard models powered by AC, 
while the CAB LiteDC is a compact model designed for integration into AGVs and powered by DC. 
JAKA |product_name| is compatible with JAKA Zu, Pro, S and AL series robots. 
Designed for seamless integration into existing infrastructure, CAB V3 delivers powerful performance across a wide range of applications. 
With a Wi-Fi board and a 4G module, it is well-suited for mobile platforms, such as AGVs and hybrid robots, and deployed in a tight workspace.
